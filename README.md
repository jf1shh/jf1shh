## Featured projects

### ClaimsRAG

<a href="https://github.com/jf1shh/claims-rag">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jf1shh/claims-rag/main/assets/demo.gif" alt="ClaimsRAG demo: a theft claim, an OEM parts rider audit, and a cited answer from a local model" width="100%">
</a>

An independent, local-first claims retrieval project, built with AI coding agents on synthetic data. It uses hybrid retrieval, reranking, claim-scoped documents and source citations, and it is scored with dated answer-quality and adversarial evaluations. A person makes the claims decisions. The README records the results and their limits. This is a portfolio proof of concept.

[Repository and evaluation](https://github.com/jf1shh/claims-rag)

### Agentic App Harness

A spec-driven framework for AI-assisted app development, with six deployed web and mobile demos. CI runs contract-first Zod schemas, behavior-driven unit and end-to-end tests, and automated accessibility checks. A deterministic loop (sense, propose, act, verify, learn) detects drift and writes work orders for a coding agent. It has no embedded LLM and needs no API key.

[Live showcase](https://jf1shh.github.io/agentic-app-harness/) · [Repository](https://github.com/jf1shh/agentic-app-harness)

### Jump Cut

A stunt-driving game where you direct the cut: drive each stunt, or hold to cut past it. My solo entry to the 2026 Epic AI MegaJam (Nov 11-15), made in Unreal Engine 5.9 with AI agents (Claude Code).

## Claims experience and AI enablement

My appraisal work covered supplement review, total loss determinations, fraud indicators, subrogation and complex shop disputes. It shapes my requirements: guidance people can use, evidence they can trace, and a clear line between what the assistant says and what a person decides.

At Liberty Mutual, I designed and piloted a RAG-based appraisal assistant and built and demoed a total-loss assistant. I also built PII scrubbing and photo PII redaction tools that were distributed to the team. I wrote a department expansion playbook, built a prompt library, and ran a cross-team training session. The assistants were pilots and demos; they were not deployed team-wide.
